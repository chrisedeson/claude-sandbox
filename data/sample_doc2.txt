Understanding Neural Networks

Neural networks are computing systems inspired by the biological neural networks in animal brains. They are a fundamental technology in deep learning and artificial intelligence. A neural network consists of interconnected nodes, called neurons, organized in layers.

Architecture:
- Input Layer: Receives the initial data
- Hidden Layers: Process the data through weighted connections
- Output Layer: Produces the final prediction or classification

Training Process:
Neural networks learn through a process called backpropagation, where the network adjusts its weights based on the error of its predictions. This iterative process continues until the network achieves acceptable accuracy.

Applications include computer vision, speech recognition, natural language understanding, and game playing. Modern neural networks can have millions of parameters and require significant computational resources for training.
